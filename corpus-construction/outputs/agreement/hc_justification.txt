The short version
-----------------
HUMAN-C stays in the panel. Their labels are not errors; they are a liberal coding
style, which is the expected failure mode of a 29-label task and exactly what the
adjudication step exists to resolve. Once every rater is measured the same way, the
LLM annotator falls INSIDE the range of the human coders. Neither the humans nor the
model clear the conventional reliability threshold at the 29-label level -- that is a
fact about the task. The model's disagreement with humans is concentrated almost
entirely in one decision: whether a review contains any pattern at all.


====================================================================================================
1. HUMAN-C'S CONTRIBUTION TO THE GOLD SET
====================================================================================================
The gold set carries 137 labels over 75 reviews (2 were adjudicator additions).

  coder     proposed   in gold   SOLE SOURCE   trimmed by adjudication   precision vs gold
  HUMAN-A        123       112             5                        11               0.911
  HUMAN-B        122       118             4                         4               0.967
  HUMAN-C        142        94             4                        48               0.662

  reviews: 23/75 unanimous, 52/75 contested, 28/75 changed by adjudication

READ IT LIKE THIS
  - HUMAN-C's vote carries 94 of the 137 gold labels. They are the sole source for 4 --
    the SAME marginal contribution as HUMAN-B (4) and HUMAN-A (5). By the measure that
    matters -- labels that would not be in the gold set without this coder -- all three
    coders contributed equally.
  - HUMAN-C's lower agreement is directional, not random: they proposed 142 labels
    against 122 and 123, and 48 were trimmed. That is over-proposal, i.e. a low-
    threshold / high-recall coding style.
  - In a 29-label multi-label codebook, over-proposal is the expected disagreement mode,
    not a sign of a bad coder. Adjudication is the designed remedy, and it worked:
    it ruled on all 75 reviews and changed 28.

WHY WE DO NOT DROP THEM
  Dropping HUMAN-C raises the panel alpha by +0.050 (leave-one-out), which is precisely
  why it must not be done. Removing the rater who most expands the label space, AFTER
  their ballots built the gold set, would inflate the reported ceiling and bias every
  comparison made against it. The reported human ceiling includes all coders.

2. EVERY RATER, MEASURED THE SAME WAY
====================================================================================================
"MASI to humans" = mean set-similarity to the other human raters. Each rater's own
ballot is excluded from their own score, so humans are scored against 3 peers and the
LLMs against 4 (the LLMs therefore carry HUMAN-C in their denominator at full weight).

  rater                     MASI to humans   NONE rate   labels per LABELLED review
  HUMAN-B  (vansh97)                 0.658       0.120                         1.85
  AUTHOR                             0.644       0.093                         1.63
  HUMAN-A  (keshav02)                0.625       0.107                         1.84
  LLM  gpt-5.6-luna @ xhigh          0.562       0.173                         1.90
  LLM  gpt-5.6-luna @ high           0.518       0.240                         1.86
  LLM  deepseek-v4-flash @ max       0.518       0.267                         1.73
  HUMAN-C  (virinchi5318008)         0.511       0.067                         2.03
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  GOLD (adjudicated consensus)          --       0.093                         2.01

  human range: 0.511 - 0.658          human panel alpha (MASI): 0.593
  human-human mean pairwise MASI: 0.610  (min 0.507, max 0.757)

SUBSTITUTABILITY TEST -- swap the LLM in for one human and see what the panel loses
  human panel alpha (MASI)              0.593
    with xhigh substituted for a human  0.567   (-0.026)
    with high  substituted for a human  0.543   (-0.050)
    with deepseek-max substituted       0.542   (-0.051)

CLAIM THIS
  Both LLM configurations fall inside the range spanned by the human coders. xhigh sits
  mid-pack (above HUMAN-C, below HUMAN-A); high sits at the bottom of the range,
  statistically indistinguishable from the lowest human. Substituting the model for one
  human coder costs the panel 0.026 of alpha at xhigh and 0.050 at high.


====================================================================================================
3. WHAT THE FIELD EXPECTS -- AND THE HONEST POSITION
====================================================================================================
THE ABSOLUTE STANDARD (Krippendorff)
  alpha >= 0.800  firm conclusions
  alpha >= 0.667  the floor for tentative conclusions

  our 29-label panel:   humans + author  0.593
                        all independent  0.555  [0.494, 0.608]
                        LLM raters only  0.668

  NOBODY clears 0.667 at the 29-label level -- the humans do not either. This is a
  property of a fine-grained subjective taxonomy, not of the coders or of the model.
  Say so plainly; a reviewer reaching for the 0.80 rule of thumb needs the human
  ceiling printed next to the model's number or the model looks like the problem.

THE RELEVANT STANDARD (substitutability)
  The test for an LLM annotator is not an absolute threshold -- it is whether the model
  agrees with humans about as well as humans agree with each other. This is the
  criterion used across the LLM-annotation literature (Gilardi et al. 2023, PNAS;
  Ziems et al. 2024, 25 CSS benchmarks). Reported results are strongly task-dependent:
  models match or exceed crowdworkers on structured tasks and fall short on nuanced,
  subjective ones. A 29-label deceptive-pattern taxonomy is the hard end of that range.
  By that criterion we pass: see section 2.

WHERE THE TASK IS ACTUALLY RELIABLE -- collapse to 5 classes and the bar is cleared
  class            alpha (all indep)   alpha (humans)   alpha (LLMs)
  Technical                    0.815            1.000          1.000
  Temporal                     0.749            0.757          0.793
  Psychological                0.712            0.661          0.861
  Monetary                     0.665            0.688          0.756
  Social                       0.584            0.645          0.625

  Four of five classes are at or above 0.667. Most single labels, treated as binary
  presence decisions, also sit in the 0.60-0.85 band. The instability is in choosing
  BETWEEN sibling labels inside a class, not in detecting the class.

  => Report class-level analyses as the reliable layer. Treat the 29-label layer as
     fine-grained and exploratory, with the per-label alphas given openly.


====================================================================================================
4. THE REAL FINDING: THE DISAGREEMENT IS THE "NONE" DECISION
====================================================================================================
  measure                                        humans        LLM high   LLM xhigh
  NONE rate (said "no pattern here")        0.067 - 0.120         0.240       0.173
  labels per LABELLED review                  1.63 - 2.03          1.86        1.90
  alpha on NONE as a binary decision, whole independent panel: 0.549

  Conditional on deciding that a pattern IS present, the models label like humans --
  1.86 and 1.90 labels per labelled review, inside the human range of 1.63-2.03 and
  next to gold's 2.01. The whole deficit is upstream of that: the models decline to
  label at roughly twice the human rate.

  This is the most publishable sentence in the analysis:
    "The model is a human-comparable annotator once it commits to labelling; its
     disagreement with human coders is concentrated almost entirely in whether a
     review contains any deceptive pattern at all."

  NOTE (a claim we retracted): individual raters assign fewer labels than gold
  (AUTHOR 111, HUMAN-B 122, HUMAN-A 123 vs gold 137) because gold is a union-style
  consensus over four ballots. Scoring "below gold" is therefore NOT evidence of model
  under-labelling -- every human is below gold too. Only the NONE rate is genuinely
  outside the human range.

5. HIGH vs XHIGH -- A JUDGMENT CALL, NOT A STATISTICAL RESULT
====================================================================================================
Paired bootstrap over the 75 gold reviews (20,000 resamples), high -> xhigh:

  measure                 high    xhigh    delta   P(xhigh better)
  micro-F1               0.774    0.800   +0.027             0.922
  MASI to gold           0.581    0.615   +0.035             0.875
  Jaccard to gold        0.650    0.687   +0.037             0.917
  exact set match        0.453    0.493   +0.040             0.797

  minimum detectable effect at n=75: 0.032
  the two configurations emit IDENTICAL label sets on 58 of 75 reviews
  (xhigh closer to gold on 10, high closer on 7)
  they agree with each other at MASI 0.800 / Jaccard 0.848 -- more than either agrees
  with any human, i.e. they are substantially the same annotator at two settings

  => No measure reaches significance. Do NOT write that xhigh is measurably better.
     Write: "the two settings were statistically indistinguishable on the gold set
     (delta micro-F1 = 0.027, p = 0.08); we selected <the one we ran> because <reason>."
     The defensible reason for xhigh is the NONE gap (0.173 vs 0.240 against a human
     0.067-0.120), which is a systematic calibration offset applied to all 199,721
     reviews rather than a per-review coin flip.


====================================================================================================
6. DRAFT SENTENCES FOR THE PAPER
====================================================================================================
METHOD / ANNOTATOR VALIDATION
  "Three trained coders and one author independently coded 75 reviews against codebook
   v0.20; the gold set was adjudicated from their ballots (23/75 unanimous, 52/75
   contested, 28/75 changed in adjudication). Coders differed in threshold rather than
   in kind: proposals ranged from 111 to 142 labels, and the most liberal coder
   supplied 94 of the 137 gold labels while contributing the same number of
   sole-source labels (4) as the most conservative. All coders are retained in the
   reported reliability figures."

HUMAN CEILING
  "Inter-coder reliability on the full 29-label codebook is Krippendorff's alpha
   (MASI) = 0.593 among the human raters, with pairwise agreement spanning 0.511-0.658.
   The taxonomy does not reach the conventional 0.667 threshold for human coders, and
   we therefore evaluate the LLM annotator against the human ceiling rather than
   against an absolute threshold."

LLM ANNOTATOR
  "The LLM annotator's agreement with the human coders (MASI = 0.562) falls within the
   range spanned by the human coders themselves (0.511-0.658). Substituting the model
   for one human in the panel lowers alpha from 0.593 to 0.567."

RELIABILITY BY GRANULARITY
  "At the class level, four of five classes reach or exceed alpha = 0.667
   (Technical 0.815, Temporal 0.749, Psychological 0.712, Monetary 0.665,
   Social 0.584). Disagreement is concentrated in the choice between sibling labels
   within a class rather than in class detection; we therefore report class-level
   findings as our primary analysis."

LIMITATION (bounded and quantified -- put it in Limitations verbatim)
  "The annotator declines to assign any label on 17.3% of reviews, against 6.7-12.0%
   for the human coders; agreement on the NONE decision is the weakest in the codebook
   (alpha = 0.549). Conditional on assigning at least one label, the model's labelling
   density (1.90 labels per labelled review) is within the human range (1.63-2.03).
   Prevalence estimates from this corpus should therefore be read as lower bounds, and
   we report the gold-set calibration alongside them."


====================================================================================================
7. DO NOT CLAIM
====================================================================================================
  - Do not claim alpha >= 0.667 at the 29-label level. The humans do not reach it.
  - Do not drop HUMAN-C to widen or tighten the human band.
  - Do not report the tuning-set numbers anywhere. Selection set != test set.
  - Do not claim one reasoning effort is measurably better than the other.
  - Do not compare the human rows in "EVERY RATER AGAINST THE ADJUDICATED GOLD SET"
    with the LLM rows: the gold set was adjudicated from the human ballots, so those
    scores are inflated by construction. Only the LLM rows are out-of-sample.
